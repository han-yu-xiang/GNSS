## Defensible novelty wording

The paper's original contribution is **this measured, traceable estimator-output cohort and its scene-held-out empirical comparison**: a Stage2-selection-conditional count/mark population over 13 road scenes, explicit within-scene dependence controls, and a fixed-observation-bin predictive check that makes a negative Copula result interpretable. This is a *study-specific combination and evidence result*, not a first GNSS multipath statistical model, first SAGE parameter estimator, first blocked cross-validation, or first satellite-channel model. Avoid “first”, “unprecedented”, “physical ground truth”, and “validated joint generator” in abstract, title and contributions.

## Remaining citation work before submission

- Confirm final venue bibliography style, all author spellings/pages and publisher full texts where accessible (especially Xie–Petovello); the V0 is not a completed systematic review.
- If a claim about GNSS-SDR operation, BIC, or a particular SAGE search rule is added, cite **this project's frozen code/configuration** as the operative evidence rather than attributing that rule to R1/R2.
- Distinguish the statistical object in every prior-study comparison: sounding CIR, receiver tracking error, static urban estimated echo, and our selected-window SAGE mark are not interchangeable.
