## Scientific status and open manuscript issues

- **PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS for the bounded V0 argument:** Stage2-estimated, selection-conditional descriptive and whole-scene predictive comparisons have an explicit denominator, source, units and direction. This is a *paper-content review*, not a change to WP or Gate acceptance.
- **Still exploratory:** environment/elevation conditions, a count-independent marked generator, Gaussian-copula dependence and code-domain prediction. Count-conditioned N=1 fitting gap, thin N=3 scene support and delay-ranked within-window dependence prevent a complete ordered-path law. Gate G stays `PENDING`.
